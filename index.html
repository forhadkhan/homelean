    Questions, answered.

    Still unsure? Message us and a real person will reply within the hour.

    How are your professionals vetted?

    Every pro passes an identity check, a background check and a skills assessment before their first job. We review ratings after each visit and remove anyone who falls below our standard.

    What if the job costs more than the quote?

    You'll never be charged more than the price you approved. If the pro finds extra work, they'll show you a new price first and you decide whether to go ahead.

    Can I reschedule or cancel?

    Yes. Reschedule or cancel for free up to 12 hours before your slot. After that, a small call-out fee may apply.

    Do you cover emergencies?

    Burst pipes and electrical faults are handled around the clock on the Whole home plan. On other plans, emergency call-outs are available at a priority rate.

    What happens if I'm not happy with the work?

    Tell us within 48 hours and we'll send someone back to put it right at no cost. If we can't fix it, you get a refund.
